# GTM brief: FY28 data leader expansion (sample)

Illustrative example only. A fictional analytics platform, Northwind.

## What we're launching
- **Northwind AI Insights** goes live in April (plan month 3; the fiscal year starts in February 2028). It lets business teams ask questions of governed data in plain language.
- The core analytics platform stays in market with always-on programs.

## The number
- Sales needs $60M in new bookings. Pipeline coverage is 3x, and marketing sources about 45% of pipeline.
- Last year marketing sourced $38M in pipeline from 3,100 MQLs.
- Average deal is about $95K in enterprise and $28K in mid-market. Enterprise is roughly 75% of the pipeline.

## Who we sell to
- Chief data officer (economic buyer): cares about trust, governance and cost.
- Head of analytics (champion): wants fewer ad hoc requests.
- Data platform engineer (technical evaluator): cares about integration and security.

## Campaigns
1. **Ask your data** (Tier 1 launch for AI Insights, about half the number). Hero offer: the State of Self-Serve Analytics report. Runs February to July.
2. **Trusted data, always on** (core platform, about 40%). Offer: ROI calculator and a customer story.
3. **Budget season** (seasonal, about 10%, October to December). Offer: a consolidation business-case workshop.

## Regions
North America leads with 60% of the number. EMEA has 30% and APJ 10%, each run by its field marketing team.

## Key moments
- April: AI Insights launch
- September: Northwind Summit (customer event)
- November: budget season
